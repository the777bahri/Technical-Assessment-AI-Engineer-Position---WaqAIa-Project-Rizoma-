# Hours and AI Assistant Use

Total time spent on this project: about 8 hours, across several days.

I used Claude throughout this project, for writing code and drafting. I directed the overall
approach, decided what to build and what to leave out given the time budget, reviewed everything
it produced, and made the final call on every tradeoff, including which model to submit.

AI is fast at writing code, so why would I not use it, the same way driving a car instead of
walking saves time.
